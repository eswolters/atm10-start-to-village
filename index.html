Hollowmere Town Plan

A medieval MineColonies town on the surface, with its high-tech core hidden underneath in an undercroft. Each step explains why it matters, then breaks into small sub-steps you tick off. Many steps also have a table of key ingredients and a sketch of the machine layout. The systems build on each other: Create, Powah, Mekanism, AE2, and finally a ComputerCraft script that fills the colony's requests from your ME system.

Planning Surface town (MineColonies) Undercroft (hidden tech)

New to automation? Read the Learn tab first: 10 minutes there makes every tech step easier. Stuck? The Fix it tab lists common problems and their causes. In game: hover an item in JEI and press R (recipe) or U (uses). Hold W over a Create item for an animated Ponder scene, and G over an AE2 item for its guide.
