Hollowmere Town Plan

A medieval MineColonies town on the surface, with its high-tech core hidden underneath in an undercroft. Each step explains why it matters, then breaks into small sub-steps you tick off. Many steps also have a table of key ingredients and a sketch of the machine layout. The systems build on each other: Create, Powah, AE2, and finally a ComputerCraft script that fills the colony's requests from your ME system. Mekanism comes later as an optional upgrade, if the colony runs short of ore. Along the way, design advice keeps the town beautiful, and the last phase is a full beautification pass.

Planning Surface town (MineColonies) Undercroft (hidden tech)

New to automation? Read the Learn tab first: 10 minutes there makes every tech step easier. Need the ingredients for something? Open the Materials tab: it lists what to gather, where to find it and how to automate it. Overwhelmed? Keep Focus on current phase ticked: only the phase you are in and its next step stay open. Time estimates are rough guesses for a first playthrough. Stuck? The Fix it tab lists common problems and their causes. In game: hover an item in JEI and press R (recipe) or U (uses). Hold W over a Create item for an animated Ponder scene, and G over an AE2 item for its guide.
